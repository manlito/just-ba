Photos of one scene, taken from different places, each overlapping others. Up to 60; every pair is matched. Of a video the page keeps the frames that overlap the last one kept by about half, as the live capture does with a camera; each video is a camera of its own, so two lenses or two resolutions can go into one model.

Settings

Reconstruct on

Pose estimation (poselib-gpu) and bundle adjustment (just-ba) run on the same one. The CPU is one thread of WebAssembly.

Features

XFeat and LighterGlue are a light pair: a 4.5 MB download instead of 24 MB, keypoints in about a third of ALIKED's time and matches in about half of LightGlue's. FlexGlue gives the best matches, from your own copy of its weights; it needs about 1 GB of GPU memory, so it is heavy on a phone.

FlexGlue's weights are not served by this page: its backbone is under Meta's DINOv3 License. Download flexglue.pth (567 MB) from FlexGlue's release and give it to the page below. It is checked, converted to half precision (284 MB) and kept in this browser, so it is needed only once (the live capture uses the same copy).
